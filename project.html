Featured Project Showcase

AI-Driven QBR Automation & Auto Risk Analysis

An initiative leveraging auto manufacturer data, risk modeling, and modern AI automation to predict high-loss vehicles, flag dealer risk, and cut client deck creation time from a full day to 10 minutes.

Context / Company Assurant (Auto Risk)
Role Auto Risk Analyst
Core Technologies Claude Code, Python, SQL, PowerBI, Tableau
Time Efficiency Gain 1 Day → 10 Minutes

Project Focus & Objectives

In automotive extended service contracts, understanding risk exposure is critical for maintaining profitable contract pricing and supporting dealer relationships. This project centered around two core objectives:

  • Predicting High-Loss Automobile Makes: Creating visualizations with publicly available auto manufacturer data to identify vehicle makes with elevated warranty claims risk, allowing data-backed adjustments to extended service contract pricing.
  • Automated Risk Identification: Leveraging Assurant Auto data to automate, identify, and flag the highest risk dealers and products, investigating possible causes behind performance variances.

AI-Driven Deck Automation with Claude Code

Preparing Quarterly Business Review (QBR) presentation decks for auto dealer clients previously required extensive manual data extraction, formatting, and chart assembly—taking up to a full day of analyst time per client.

To solve this bottleneck, I built an AI-driven automation skill using Claude Code and structured datasets. The solution parses structured risk, volume, and performance data directly into presentation-ready QBR decks, reducing deck production time from a full working day to just 10 minutes.

Key Outcomes & Value Delivered

• Over 95% reduction in report generation latency (from ~8 hours to 10 minutes).

• Enhanced consistency across client-facing dealer review decks.

• Proactive identification of high-loss vehicle makes before contract profitability is impacted.
